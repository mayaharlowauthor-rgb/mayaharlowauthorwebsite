About

Meet Maya Harlow

I write romance and fantasy with emotional depth, immersive worlds, and unforgettable love stories.

I've always been drawn to books that offer escape, intensity, wonder, and the kind of connection that lingers long after the final page. Whether through fantasy, romance, or stories still to come, I love creating worlds readers can fall into completely.

Treasured by the Dragon King is my debut novel and the first book in the Blackridge Universe.

I sincerely hope you enjoy living in my world as much as I do.

“For all the believers and dreamers out there: I hope you find the courage to keep following what calls to you, and that along the way, you find others travelling the same path.”
